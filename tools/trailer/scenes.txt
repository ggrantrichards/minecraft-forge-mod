# The Astral End - the trailer's edit. Read by the game (dev/ClientTrailer.java films each shot) and
# by tools/trailer/compose.py (cuts it together with the titles and the score). One beat per line:
#
#   card <seconds> | <text>                  words on black between shots ("\" breaks a line)
#   logo <seconds>                           the title card at the end
#   <shot> <frames> [key=value ...] | <caption>
#
# <shot> is a viewpoint the worldgen survey chose (astralend-shots.txt). The camera starts there and
# moves over <frames> frames (24 a second):
#   fwd, side, up     blocks to travel forward (along the starting view), to the right, and up
#   yaw               degrees to pan (positive: to the right); pitch=a:b tilts from a to b
#   orbit             degrees to circle the focus (positive: counter-clockwise from above)
#   focus             what to orbit and look at: the shot's mob (default), or "hit" (where the view
#                     first meets the ground)
#   track             blocks per frame that the camera and the mob glide along together
#   roll              degrees of camera roll, eased in over the shot
#   ease              both (default), in, out or none
#   comet             a live comet crosses the sky and comes down ahead; the camera follows it
# and things to happen: wake@N, bridges@N (on frame N), flare=<phase>, clear.
# The caption after "|" is shown small in the corner; leave it empty for none.
