# Recording the trailer's narration

The trailer runs on title cards alone until there are recordings here. To narrate it, record each line
below as its own file and name it by its number: `00.wav`, `01.wav`, ... (`.wav`, `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.ogg` or
`.flac`). Skip any line you like; it's just left out.

Tips: a quiet room, the phone or mic a hand's width away, a second of silence before and after each line.
Speak slowly and low, with long pauses where the `...` are. The cleanup (rumble cut, levels evened, a
touch of room) is automatic; the pitch isn't changed.
