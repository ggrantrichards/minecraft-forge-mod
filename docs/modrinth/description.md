# The Astral End

**The End was never empty. It was waiting.** Beyond 1,000 blocks from the dragon's island, the outer End
becomes a sparse sky of drifting islands: quiet, alien, full of ruins no one is left to explain, and as
dangerous as the void beneath them.

> **Start a new world**, or explore beyond 1,000 blocks of an old End that hasn't been generated yet:

## Built on six ideas

The void and emptiness. A restricted palette, the End's own cream and purple. Displacement. Silence and
alienness. Ruins without explanation. Finality. No new armour tiers, no friendly villagers, no text in
your face: the End tells you things with sound and light.

## The islands

- **Nebula Fields:** an emission nebula you can walk in - the gas clouds glowing across its sky, rose
  Nebula Moss, Drift Bladders of glowing gas floating over it on their tethers, groves of magenta reeds
  whose tips glow cold blue, low drifting haze, Pollinators, and low gravity.
- **Deep Hollows:** the End's only forest, dark Nullwood on ring-shaped islands, hung with Hollow
  Vines whose black thorns carve pumpkins into violet Hollow Lanterns; Upwells beneath. Under it all, the **Hollow Roots**: caves through the dead roots, lit
  by glowcaps and amber Starsap, where fireflies drift.
- **Glimmering Crags:** dark Nightstone peaks grown through with cold, glowing Glimmer Crystal, and root caves where the Umbral
  Stalker waits. Its variant, the **Shattered Crags**, is split into shards over the void; below them
  drift the **Crystal Vaults**, geodes of crystal where a **Shardback** waits in each, folded down
  among the clusters and looking like one of them.
- **Ashen Barrens:** the leviathans' graveyard - a pale, suffocating mist of ash over stone shelves
  strewn with ribcages, vertebrae and lone ribs, porous bone-white spires and the withered stalks of
  dead giant mushrooms; Memory Moss replays what passed over it.

## The Gilded Reach

Rare, far out, and unlike anything else in the End: one great disc of **golden meadow**, the Gilded Reach. Gold turf and gold grass under
amber fog, gold dust in the air, teal water in the **Wellspring** at its heart and in ponds ringed with
green moss, outcrops of bare End Stone with Sunscale Lichen, a few clumps of orange-red Flareblooms, and
giant Lumineshrooms, cream-gold with teal spots. Overhead, clouds of gold and blue, and a white star with
six long spikes - the star the **Sunstorms** break from. When it brightens, get under a Lumineshroom.
Only here do the stars show without a spyglass, and only from here can you sight the constellations the
Starforge needs.

## The creatures

- **Drift Whales** travel in pods toward the Gilded Reach, no two alike - big leaders, calves by their
  mothers, curious ones and shy ones - answering each other's hums, rolling and breaching at play. Sing
  one a song (Memory Moss, a note block, Chorus Fruit) and it's yours to ride.
- **Hushbacks**, dark, clear-hided grazers whose herd blinks away when one is hurt; **Hydragrips**, dark
  newts lit with cyan, which hunt endermites by the water; and, in the Ashen Barrens, the **Mirror Wretch**, a
  slithering worm with a round toothed mouth and a vocal sac for every voice it has stolen, calling you in
  voices you know from the void's edge - the Endermen at the edges stand listening.
- **The Watcher** is only ever seen from the corner of your eye. Look straight at it and it teleports
  away - and turns up further along, leading you to the Leviathan.
- The **Umbral Stalker**, unseen in the Crags' caves until it hunts; the **Shardback**, a crystal-backed
  ambusher in the Crystal Vaults; and Pollinators, spreading the Nebula Fields' glowing plants.

### They live with each other

Every creature has its threads: Hushback herds keep a lookout that chimes when something comes (sneak
up on them); Hydragrips snap up the endermites that pester the herds and lounge on their backs; whale
calves chase Pollinators until their mothers fetch them home, pods sleep hanging nose-up in the void
and circle a Leviathan when they pass one; a Mirror Wretch hums at whales in their own voice, and they
go silent and steer away; a wolf growls where an Umbral Stalker hides; Endermen won't meet the
Watcher's eye. When a Sunstorm comes, everything alive takes cover.

## The Leviathan

The whole skeleton of a Star Leviathan, some 370 blocks from tail to chin, adrift high over the void -
and the purpur citadel someone built on it: a hall along its spine, towers on its vertebrae, a
Constellation Maze on the last of them. Its heart still beats, crimson, in the ribcage, its lungs of
Breathcomb still breathe light, and its stomach holds what it last swallowed. The ribcage is a
cathedral now, the reliquary, kept by **the Curator**, a hulking war-construct of the builders: it
raises its cannon arm and locks on before it fires, crouches before it charges (dodge, and it stuns
itself on a wall or a pylon), and at half health its glass case shatters and **the whole fight turns
over** - everyone in the reliquary falls up onto its ceiling, and the Curator follows. Its **Starforge
Templates** are the only ones there are: defeat it to starforge. Set the
dragon egg on the pedestal under the heart and the skull's eyes light - and the skull itself is the
**Maw**: teeth like trees, a hall where its brain was, and a throat that wakes into a wormhole to
every other Leviathan you've woken.

In its lower ribs roam **Hollowbacks**: they look like Hushbacks, calm and slow, until one is on you -
then its head splits open into a jaw full of fangs.

## Ruins and Starforging

The Leviathans' **Maws**, which wake into a travel network, and the **Constellation Maze** on each
one's last tower, whose bridges show only through a spyglass; the Solar Array's mirrors in the meadow;
the Shattered Observatory and the Comet Craters. Bring back what they guard to
an **Astral Forge**: five sidegrades for diamond and netherite gear - Void Recall, Echo Strike, Lensing,
Radiant, Rift Walker - each needing a Starforge Template from the Curator, its constellation sighted
from the Gilded Reach, and a rare material from somewhere else.
