# The Astral End

**The End is alive.** This mod adds new biomes, creatures and structures to the outer End, the islands
more than 1,000 blocks from the dragon's island.

> **Start a new world**, or go past 1,000 blocks in an old world where the End hasn't been explored yet.
> Worlds from the betas don't carry over. Back up any world you care about.

**Works on Minecraft 26.3 (Forge 66) and 1.21.1 (Forge 52.1 or newer).** Download the file for your
version.

**[Field guide](https://ggrantrichards.github.io/the-astral-end/)**: pictures of every biome, structure
and creature, and how the creatures behave around each other.

## Biomes

- **Nebula Fields:** pink moss, tall magenta reeds and floating Drift Bladders, with glowing gas clouds
  in the sky. Gravity is lower here.
- **Deep Hollows:** a dark forest of Nullwood trees on ring-shaped islands. Hollow Vines hang from the
  trees, and their thorns carve pumpkins into Hollow Lanterns. Upwells flip gravity. Underneath are the
  **Hollow Roots**, caves full of roots, glowing mushrooms and Starsap ore.
- **Glimmering Crags:** dark Nightstone peaks with Glimmer Crystal growing out of them, and caves where
  the Umbral Stalker lives. The **Shattered Crags** are a version broken into small floating pieces, with
  the **Crystal Vaults** underneath: floating geodes, each with a Shardback hiding inside.
- **Ashen Barrens:** flat pale stone covered in falling ash, with old Leviathan bones lying around.
  Memory Moss grows here.
- **The Gilded Reach:** a rare disc of golden grassland with ponds, a spring in the middle and giant
  Lumineshrooms. The star above it causes Sunstorms. When the sky brightens, get under a Lumineshroom.
  It's also the only place you can see the constellations needed for Starforging.

## Creatures

- **Drift Whales** travel in pods toward the Gilded Reach. Tame one with Memory Moss, a note block and
  Chorus Fruit, and you can ride it.
- **Hushbacks** graze in herds. Hurt one and the whole herd teleports away. Shear them for Hushfleece.
- **Hydragrips** live by the ponds and hunt endermites, dropping an Ender Pearl for each one.
- **Mirror Wretches** live in the Ashen Barrens. They copy familiar sounds near island edges and attack
  when you get close.
- **Watchers** teleport away when you look straight at them, always a bit closer to the nearest
  Leviathan.
- **Umbral Stalkers** are invisible in the dark until they attack. They're afraid of fire.
- **Shardbacks** hide in geodes looking like a crystal cluster.
- **Pollinators** spread the Nebula Fields' glowing plants.

The creatures react to each other. Hushback herds keep a lookout. Hydragrips go after the endermites
that follow the herds. Whale calves chase Pollinators until their mothers bring them back. A wolf will
growl at a hidden Stalker. When a Sunstorm comes, the animals take cover.

## The Leviathan

A giant skeleton, about 370 blocks long, with End City towers built on it. Its heart still beats.
The ribcage holds the reliquary, guarded by **the Curator**, a large robot mini-boss. It fires a beam
and charges at you. At half health its glass case breaks and the room flips upside down. It drops the
**Starforge Templates**, which you can't get anywhere else.

Put a dragon egg on the pedestal under the heart and the skull wakes up. The skull is a portal to every
other Leviathan you've woken. **Hollowbacks** roam the lower ribs. They look like Hushbacks until they
get close.

## Structures and Starforging

There's also the Constellation Maze on the Leviathan's last tower (its bridges only show through a
spyglass), the Solar Array, the Shattered Observatory and the Comet Craters.

The **Astral Forge** adds five upgrades for diamond and netherite gear: Void Recall, Echo Strike,
Lensing, Radiant and Rift Walker. Each one needs a Starforge Template, its constellation seen from the
Gilded Reach, and a rare material. Starforged armour glows in the upgrade's colour.

## Other things

A **Lodestar** respawn point for the End. Four music tracks, a music disc, ambient sounds for each
biome, and advancements.
