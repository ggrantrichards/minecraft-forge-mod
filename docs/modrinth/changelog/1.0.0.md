**The Astral End 1.0.** Out of beta: everything from beta.5, finished.

- **Start a new world** (or explore an old End beyond what's been generated): worlds from the betas don't
  carry over.
- **Everything can be found in survival now.** The music disc *Observing the Star* waits in the
  Constellation Maze's chest. The Gilded Armor Trim's template turns up in the Solar Array. The Spire
  and Wyrm banner patterns are in the Shattered Observatory's vault and the Leviathan's hold. All of
  them used to be only in the retired Resonant Spire.
- **Hollowbacks** drop a tuft of Hushfleece as well as bones.
- The Eclipse Armor Trim is now the **Gilded Armor Trim**, after the biome it comes from.
- The Mods screen shows the right version and a description of what's actually in the mod.
- Also for **Minecraft 1.21.1** (Forge 52.1 or newer): pick the file for your version.
