**Polish.** Fixes and reworks from playing it, checked by a new set of automated tests that play the
game: they use every spawn egg, light a hand-built portal, fight the Spire Wyrm, ride a whale and
cross biome borders. Explore new parts of the End to see the new structures and terrain.

**New and reworked**
- **The Remnant is real.** It used to be only a glow in the sky that slid away as you flew toward it.
  Now a cracked, glowing star-cinder 40 blocks across hangs high over every Corona, ringed by broken
  rock and loops of fire. Two rifts lead in to the chamber at its heart, and its chest.
- **The Corona** has Solarite spires and scorch fissures glowing with magma.
- **Rift Maws are colossal.** The skull is 60 blocks across, with an 80-block snout and jaw and teeth as
  long as trees. Inside the cranium is a hall with galleries, ladders, light and an explorers' camp.
- **The Spire Wyrm fight is rebuilt.** It has its own voice, sleeps on its nest on the tower top, stays at
  its spire, dives onto the platform and lies grounded where you can hit it. Its slam staggers you
  instead of flinging you off.
- **Upwelling turns you upside down:** you stand on the underside of whatever is above you and walk
  there, head down.
- **Biomes blend:** the sky shifts gently across borders, and borders interlock (vanilla biomes too).
- **Ambience:** each astral biome has its own quiet background sound.
- **Oases** in the Nebula Fields; the **Chorus Thicket** grows in groves with clearings.
- **Constellation Mazes** use a vanilla **spyglass** (one waits on the first platform); the Maze
  Telescope block is gone.
- **Drift Whales:** you sit on the whale's back, it turns smoothly, and the third-person camera stays clear
  of its body.

**Fixed**
- The Umbral Stalker spawn egg did work, but the Stalker is invisible until it hunts. Creative and
  spectator players now see it half-shown.
- About End portals that won't light: the mod doesn't touch them. Vanilla only lights a portal whose
  twelve frames all face into the ring, and a frame faces the player who places it. So place the frames
  while standing inside the ring.
