**The distillation.** The mod is cut back to six ideas - void and emptiness, a restricted palette,
displacement, silence and alienness, ruins without explanation, and finality - and rebuilt around them.
**Start a new world:** this beta isn't compatible with beta.2 worlds.

**New**
- **The Eclipse:** a rare disc of land, a yin-yang of heaven (bare End Stone, the teal sky, moss by its
  water, the Wellspring) and hell (crimson stone and sky, Sunstorms, the Remnant overhead). Its mobs:
  the Hushback, the Hydragrip and the Mirror Wretch.
- **The Leviathan:** a 320-block skeleton with a citadel built on it, its garrison, the Hollowbacks,
  and in its skull the reliquary of **the Curator**, a miniboss. The dragon egg has a pedestal there.
- **Variants:** the Deep Hollows (Hollow Isles) and the Shattered Crags (Glimmering Crags). The Crags
  have root caves, and the Umbral Stalker lives in them.
- **Systems:** Memory Moss and the Mimic Plant; Drift Whale pods, tamed by song; the Watcher as a guide
  to leviathan remains; the Lodestar as the End's respawn point; purpur bricks and walls; a void hum
  near island edges; endermen standing at the Ashen Barrens' edges.
- **Starforging** is five sidegrades, each needing a constellation sighted from the Eclipse and a rare
  material: Void Recall (the Curator's Core), Echo Strike, Lensing, Radiant and Rift Walker.
- The Watcher's eye is the Hourglass Nebula.

**Changed**
- The sky is vanilla's black again; stars, auroras and constellations show only over the Eclipse (and
  through a spyglass anywhere). No more text messages: the Rift Maws, mazes and Star Charts speak in
  sound and light.
- Sparser islands with wider gaps. Glimmer Crystal is smoky violet.
- Nullwood is the End's one wood. Lumineshrooms grow in the Eclipse, pale purpur and sparse.

**Removed**
- The Comet Wake biome, live comets and comet showers, the Cinder Mite, the Live Comet and the Hollow;
  the Chorus and Lumine wood sets; the Heat Shield and Time Stretch upgrades. The Resonant Spire and the
  Spire Wyrm are out of the world (for now).

**Also since beta.2:** the Remnant is a real structure; Rift Maws are colossal skulls with halls
inside; biome borders blend; each biome has its own ambience; Constellation Mazes use a vanilla
spyglass; Upwelling turns you upside down; Drift Whale riding is smoother.
