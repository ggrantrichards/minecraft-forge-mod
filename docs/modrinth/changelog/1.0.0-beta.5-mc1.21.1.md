**The Astral End on Minecraft 1.21.1.** This is beta.5, built for Minecraft 1.21.1 and Forge 52.1 or newer:
every biome, structure, creature and system of the 26.3 version.

- **Start a new world.** As with every beta, worlds from other versions aren't compatible.
- **"Experimental Settings" warning:** Minecraft 1.21.1 shows it for any world whose world generation
  comes from a mod, as the Astral End's End does. Choose "I know what I'm doing!" to play.
- The End's islands are laid out differently from a 26.3 world with the same seed (the regions are just
  as large, in other places).
- The Hollow Roots' fireflies are glowing motes here (1.21.1 has no firefly particle).
