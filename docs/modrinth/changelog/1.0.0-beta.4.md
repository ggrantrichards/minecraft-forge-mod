**One Leviathan, two caves, and a living void.** From playing beta.3. **Start a new world:** this beta
isn't compatible with beta.3 worlds.

**New**
- **The Leviathan, whole:** the Rift Maw's colossal skull is the Leviathan's head now - its teeth, the
  hall where its brain was, a face of brows, eye sockets, cheekbones and nostrils, and the throat that
  wakes into a wormhole between every Leviathan you've woken. In its ribcage its organs still live: a
  crimson **Leviathan Heart** beating slowly over the egg's pedestal, Breathcomb lungs, a Voidgel
  stomach with something inside. The Curator's reliquary is the ribcage cathedral; a **Constellation
  Maze** crowns its last tower. It floats high over the void, clear of every island.
- **The Hollow Roots**, a cave biome under the Hollow Isles: Hollow Glowcaps, hanging roots, **Starsap**
  (amber ore, long-burning fuel, a glowing block) and the **Gloamgrub**, a harmless glowing grub.
- **The Crystal Vaults**, a cave biome under the Shattered Crags: drifting geodes of Glimmer Crystal, each
  with a **Shardback** folded down among the crystals, waiting.
- **The Hollow Thorn**, from the Deep Hollows' vines: carves pumpkins into violet **Hollow Lanterns**.
- **Drift Whales** are each their own: size, pace, temper. Calves keep by their mothers; they answer each
  other's hums, and play - rolls, breaching loops, spiral chases.

**Changed**
- The **Mirror Wretch** is reborn as a rearing worm with a round toothed mouth and a vocal sac for every
  voice it has stolen; the **Hydragrip** as a frilled, frog-eyed Gripmouth.
- **Biomes blend:** ground mixes across borders, borders weave in wider lobes, fog eases between biomes.
- Lighter Glimmering Crags and Deep Hollows; rare Ashen Barrens bones and craters (and none floating);
  the Wellspring sits in stone; the Solar Array stands on a lava-ringed mesa.
- Summoned Watchers stay where you put them.
- **End-native blocks only:** the Nether blocks are gone from End structures (Leviathan Flesh for the
  heart's muscle; raw Solarite, which now glows and holds fire forever, for magma and shroomlight) - all
  but the Remnant's magma, blackstone and basalt, the cinders of a dead star.
- The Mirror Wretch rears over two blocks tall.

**Fixed**
- Upwelling: you can look around while upside down.
- The Leviathan's shulkers stay on its towers.

**Removed**
- The Rift Maw and the Whalefall (both live on in the Leviathan).
