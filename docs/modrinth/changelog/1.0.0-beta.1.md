The first public build of The Astral End.

- Six new biomes in the outer End: Nebula Fields, Aurora Shallows, the Corona, Comet Wake, the Dark Matter Belt and the Shattered Observatory.
- New creatures: the Drift Whale, Void Wanderer, End Pollinator, Corona Skimmer, Umbral Stalker and more.
- A living sky: turning stars, auroras, the Remnant, and Stellar Flares over the Corona.
- Live comets you can ride with an elytra.
- Ruins and structures: Observatory Ruins and their vaults, Wormhole Maws, Constellation Mazes, Abandoned Orreries, Whalefalls and more.
- Starforging, new ores and materials, and whole new block families.

**This is a beta.** Blocks and items may be renamed, reworked or removed before 1.0. Back up worlds you care about.
