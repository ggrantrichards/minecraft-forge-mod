**The rework.** A big beta: much of the mod has been reworked toward what a real Minecraft End update
would ship. **Back up your worlds:** several blocks and items were renamed or removed, and the biome
layout of the outer End changed, so explore new parts of the End to see the new content.

**New**
- Two new biomes: the **Chorus Thicket** (chorus trees of glassy Chorus Wood, a full wood set) and the
  **Glimmering Crags** (jagged Nightstone peaks and teal Glimmer Crystal; Void Crystal now grows only
  in the bottoms of these islands).
- **Resonant Spires** and their mini-boss, the **Spire Wyrm** - ring the bells to bring it down. Its
  Resonant Key opens the **Resonance Vault**: two banner patterns, the Eclipse armor trim and a music disc.
- **The Whalefall** is now a colossal landmark adrift in the void, built into by those who came after.
- **Potion of Upwelling** (from Upwell Motes), **Voidgel**, **End Chimes**, the **Lodestar**, and the
  **Chorus Flower on a Stick** to ride Drift Whales.
- **Music** for the astral biomes and the disc "Observing the Star" (all free to use; credits in the mod).

**Changed**
- Renamed: Dark Matter Belt → Hollow Isles, Aurora Shallows → Ashen Barrens, Black Hole Guardian → The
  Hollow, Corona Skimmer → Void Ray, Singularity Shard → Abyss Shard, Wormhole Maw → Rift Maw, gravity
  inversion pockets → Upwells, Stellar Flares → Sunstorms, Umbral Stone → Nightstone, Nebula Moss →
  Chorus Moss.
- The Shattered Observatory is a structure now, not a biome. The Ashen Barrens are a dead biome.
- Drift Whales can be ridden (and killed - they never fight back). The Umbral Stalker senses you through
  walls, snuffs out lights (with mobGriefing on) and flees fire; it no longer throws you toward the void.
- Starforge upgrades are smaller; the alloy needs Dragon's Breath; templates also turn up in End Cities.
- Comets bring blue ice; one Charged Void Crystal lights two Rift Maw eyes; chorus and Endermen live
  only in the living biomes.

**Removed**
- The Abandoned Orrery and everything clockwork (Orrery Bronze and its family, the Orrery Heart,
  Shutters, Lamp and Sentinel), and Comet Ice.
