**The Astral End 1.0, for Minecraft 1.21.1** (Forge 52.1 or newer). Out of beta, and the same as the
Minecraft 26.3 version.

- **Start a new world** (or explore an old End beyond what's been generated): worlds from the betas don't
  carry over.
- **No more "Experimental Settings" question.** Minecraft 1.21.1 asks it for any mod that adds world
  generation, and the mod now answers it for you.
- **Everything can be found in survival now.** The music disc *Observing the Star* is in the
  Constellation Maze's chest. The Gilded Armor Trim's template is in the Solar Array. The Spire
  and Wyrm banner patterns are in the Shattered Observatory's vault and the Leviathan's hold.
- **Starforged armour glows** in the upgrade's colour, instead of having coloured pixels painted on.
- **Hollowbacks** drop a tuft of Hushfleece as well as bones.
- The Eclipse Armor Trim is now the **Gilded Armor Trim**, after the biome it comes from.
- The Mods screen shows the right version and an up-to-date description.
