**Fewer, fuller biomes, a Curator to fear, and a fight that turns over.** **Start a new world:** this beta
isn't compatible with beta.4 worlds.

**New**
- **The Gilded Reach** (the Eclipse, made over): one great disc of golden meadow - gold turf and grass,
  teal water in the Wellspring and its ponds, rolling hills and rocky knolls, giant Lumineshrooms, and
  a six-spiked star overhead, the one the Sunstorms break from (get under a Lumineshroom). The Solar
  Array stands in the meadow. Its hell half, the Seam and the Remnant are gone.
- **The Nebula Fields are an emission nebula:** rose Nebula Moss, magenta reed groves with cold-blue
  tips, Drift Bladders of glowing gas tethered over the moss, drifting haze, and great glowing gas
  clouds across the sky.
- **The Ashen Barrens are the Leviathans' graveyard:** whole ribcages, vertebrae and lone ribs, porous
  bone-white spires, withered giant stalks, grey Ashen Grass and Reeds, a pale mist of ash - and the
  Mirror Wretch, with Solarite glowing in scorch fissures.
- **The Curator, rebuilt** as a hulking war-construct: obsidian and gunmetal plate, massive pauldrons, a
  violet visor slit, its core behind glass, a cannon for an arm. Every attack shows before it lands:
  it raises the cannon and locks on before the beam; it crouches, thrusters flaring, before it charges
  (dodge, and it stuns itself on a wall or a pylon); up close it punches, left and right.
- **The fight turns over:** at half health the Curator's case shatters and everyone in the reliquary
  falls up onto its ceiling, where four more pylons hang. The Curator follows, faster, but its bare
  core takes more harm. When it dies, gravity comes back gently.
- **Starforging needs the Curator:** Starforge Templates drop only from it (two per kill: one to use,
  one to copy), as netherite's template comes only from bastions. Other chests give Starforge Alloy.

**Changed**
- **The Leviathan looks like Minecraft:** separate vertebrae with tall spines, ribs that sweep back,
  a sloped forehead, darker and browner weathered bone in broad patches, End City towers with crowns,
  a hall with purpur pillars and a pitched roof, End Rods along its ridge and crest.
- **Eight mobs and a mini-boss:** the Drift Whale, Pollinator, Hushback, Hydragrip, Mirror Wretch,
  Umbral Stalker, Watcher and Shardback, the Hollowback, and the Curator.
- Sunshades are made from Hushfleece, and Sun Ward is brewed from Sunscale Lichen. "Harvest Moon" is now
  shearing a Hushback; "Hold Still" is now "Through the Lens" (make a Portal Lens).
- Cracked Lenses and Chart Fragments come from the builders' chests.
- **The Hollow Isles are folded into the Deep Hollows**, now a biome of their own, lit by many more Null
  Lanterns, with the Umbral Stalker under the canopy. The Glimmering Crags glow a cold white-lavender.
- Mirror Wretches no longer attack Endermen; the Endermen at the Ashen Barrens' edges turn to face one.

**Fixed**
- The Collector Pedestal can be crafted again (Sunscorched Stone smelts into Pale End Stone).

**Removed**
- The Chorus Tangle (the Nebula Fields take its place).
- The Void Wanderer (too like the Watcher) and its Wanderer Relay; the Void Ray and Ray Film.
- Starforge Templates in End City treasure.
