# The Astral End, distilled

This is the plan that replaces the sprawl of Phases 1-11 with a smaller, sharper mod. It keeps the
engine (world generation, structures, sky, sounds, the CI that plays the game) and a good part of the
content, but re-cuts the content so it protects the End's six pillars. Where this file and
[END_UPDATE.md](END_UPDATE.md) disagree, this file wins.

## The six pillars

| Pillar | How it shows up in vanilla | Rule for the mod |
|---|---|---|
| **Void and emptiness** | A black, starless sky; huge gaps between islands; a fall loses everything | Biomes stay sparse. Danger comes from edges and falling. |
| **Restricted palette** | Pale end stone, black obsidian, lilac purpur, magenta chorus | Cold violet, black and bone. No greens, no warm colours - except as a deliberate opposite (the Eclipse). |
| **Displacement** | Endermen, pearls, chorus fruit, gateways, shulker levitation | Every mob and mechanic involves teleportation, gravity or the void. |
| **Silence and alienness** | No day or night, no weather, beds explode, sparse sound | Ambience gets richer but stays quiet. Life is strange, never cozy. |
| **Ruins without explanation** | End Cities, ships, shulkers guarding loot | Lore lives only in the world: no text, no NPCs. |
| **Finality** | The dragon, the credits, the poem, the egg | The dragon fight is untouched. Everything new is the aftermath. |

**Guardrails:** no armour tier above netherite; the elytra-versus-chestplate trade-off is untouched;
no friendly NPCs; no lush or water content except the Eclipse, which exists to be the opposite.

**The feel we want:** the player is never comfortable in the End, but playing it is satisfying,
adventurous and full of mystery.

## The shape of it

Four biomes keep every pillar. The fifth, **the Eclipse**, breaks them on purpose and keeps them at
the same time: heaven and hell in one place. All lore is environmental, and it's all about death and
what's left over: the leviathans that died and were built into, the forest that died and crystallised,
the star that died and still burns.

## Biomes: 5 core, 3 variants

New biomes only appear beyond the gateway ring (1,000+ blocks out), among the vanilla Highlands,
Midlands and Barrens. Borders interlock, and the fog, sky tint and ambience shift over 10-15 blocks.

### Hollow Isles - the End's only forest *(variant: Deep Hollows)*
- Ring-shaped islands of Nightstone, with tendrils hanging from their undersides, and Upwells
  where gravity points up.
- **Nullwood** forests: dark, sparse, the trunks about an enderman's height, so you can't always tell
  a trunk from an enderman. Endermen spawn more here.
- **Mimic Plant**: a dark shrub that copies nearby sounds - footsteps, an enderman's teleport, a
  Void Wanderer's hum. It makes no sound of its own. The forest feels watched.
- *Deep Hollows:* denser and darker, vines hanging to the ground, more Mimic Plants.

### Nebula Fields - the open violet plains *(variant: Chorus Tangle)*
- Sparse rolling Chorus Moss, small Lumineshrooms, Nebula Tufts. **Low gravity**: you fall slower and
  jump higher.
- Void Wanderers drift here.
- *Chorus Tangle:* dense, tangled thickets of vanilla chorus (no wood). Hard to cross, easy to fall from.

### Glimmering Crags - where the old forest died *(variant: Shattered Crags)*
- Jagged peaks that are the petrified trunks of colossal Nullwood that died long ago, grown through
  with smoky violet crystal (Glimmer Crystal, recoloured from teal).
- **The End's first caves:** inside the larger islands, crystal tunnels that follow the old root
  paths and taper into narrow channels. Pitch dark and quiet. **Void Crystal**, the rarest ore, grows
  here. The **Umbral Stalker** hunts here - something on the ceiling is not a crystal.
- *Shattered Crags:* fields of small fragments, flyable only with elytra. Void Rays circle the gaps.

### Ashen Barrens - the leviathan graveyard *(no variant)*
- Flat pale islands, ash in the air, the palest sky tint. **Void streaks** (pale lines falling far
  away) are seen most often here, and old **impact craters** hold Meteoric Iron.
- **Memory Moss**: pale grey-violet moss. Brush or trample it and for a few seconds faint
  afterimages show what once stood there.
- Endermen stand at island edges, motionless, facing the void. The game never says why.
- The **Watcher** is seen here, and the leviathans lie here.

### The Eclipse - heaven and hell *(no variant)*
Very rare (about one in every 5,000 blocks) and 300-400 blocks across, laid out like a yin-yang: two
halves with an S-shaped border, each holding a seed of the other.
- **Heaven half:** at its heart the **Wellspring**, a disc of water hovering over an open hole into the
  void (swim in it and look down past your feet into nothing). Pools, small waterfalls spilling off
  the edges into mist, living turf, giant **Lumineshrooms** (their only home), and **Breathcomb**, which
  opens and closes over about five minutes, so the light breathes. The only place with stars,
  auroras and the constellations. Endermen never spawn here; one that teleports in panics and leaves.
- **Hell half:** the scorched ground of the old Corona - sunscorched stone, Solarite, Flareblooms,
  spires, fissures glowing with magma - and above it **the Remnant**, the cinder of a dead star.
  Its **Sunstorms** burn anyone under open sky in this half; in the heaven half the plants' light
  swells instead. Sun Ward and the Sunshade belong here.
- **The seeds:** one burnt spire stands in the oasis; one small living pool lies in the scorched half.
- The **Mirror Wretch** prowls the border between the halves.
- Still the End: it floats over the void, beds explode, there's no sun but the dead one.

## The Leviathan

The mod's great landmark: the skeleton of a colossal Star Leviathan, 300-400 blocks long, that someone
long ago turned into a citadel. It drifts in the void near the Ashen Barrens.

- **The frame** is vanilla bone block: skull, spine and ribs, readable as a creature from 500 blocks.
- **Built over it:** purpur and End-stone-brick halls along the spine, towers rising from the
  vertebrae, the ribs closed into a vaulted hall with glass between them, the jaw chained shut with
  obsidian around a captured island, crying obsidian in the eye sockets, End rods, banners, chests.
- **Parts:** the ribcage cathedral, the spine city, the jaw and its island, and the skull reliquary.
- **Garrisoned, never lived in:** shulkers on the towers, Void Wanderers (the builders' sentinels)
  in the halls, and in the dark lower ribs the **Hollowbacks** - Hushbacks the builders experimented
  on. Watchers keep their distance outside.
- **The Rift Maws** stay as smaller leviathan skulls, the travel network.

## The miniboss: the Curator

In the Leviathan's skull reliquary. A 4.5-block-tall enderman-like construct of obsidian plating and
purpur inlay, no face, one slit of violet light, hovering. Dormant until you enter; one per Leviathan;
never respawns.
- **Arena:** a floor broken by void gaps, four End Chimes pylons at the edges.
- **Phase Step:** dodges projectiles by teleporting, strikes when it reappears.
- **Pylons:** ring a pylon and it stills teleports near it; the Curator can then be hit by arrows and
  tridents, so it smashes rung pylons first. (The dragon is healed by its crystals; the Curator is
  weakened by its pylons.)
- **Gravity Toll:** a slam whose ring gives 2 seconds of Levitation. Over the gaps, deadly; for mace
  users, free height.
- **Swap:** break line of sight for about 3 seconds and it swaps places with you.
- **Water weakness:** a splash water bottle staggers it for 2 seconds.
- **Drops:** the Curator's Core, and a lot of XP.
- **The egg pedestal:** placing the Dragon Egg on it changes nothing, but the skull's eyes light up,
  the Watchers outside gather facing it, and you earn an advancement.

The Spire Wyrm and the Resonant Spire are out of the world (their code and model are kept, unused).

## Mobs: 9 and the Curator

| Mob | Home | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Drift Whale** | the open void, in pods of 3-5 | Huge, translucent, cold-glowing. Pods migrate between islands and visit the Eclipse, so following one is how you find it. Eats chorus. **Tamed by song:** eat Memory Moss, play a note block (it turns and sings back), feed it chorus. Ridden with a Chorus Flower on a Stick; slow, so it never replaces the elytra. |
| **Void Ray** | Shattered Crags | A dark manta circling the gaps. Glide through its wake for a short boost. |
| **Umbral Stalker** | Glimmering Crags caves | Unseen until it hunts; senses you through walls; snuffs out lights; fears fire. |
| **Void Wanderer** | Nebula Fields, the Leviathan | A purpur sentinel that studies you and blinks away. Its lens drops only when it dies. |
| **Watcher** | Ashen Barrens | Seen only at a distance; fades when you look straight at it. Kept in the corner of your eye, it leads you to leviathan remains. |
| **Pollinator** | the Eclipse | Blinks from bloom to bloom. |
| **Hushback** | the Eclipse | A translucent herd grazer, its bones showing. When one is hurt the whole herd teleports away. Food and fleece. *Variant:* the **Hollowback** of the Leviathan, a hostile, hollowed-out Hushback. |
| **Hydragrip** | the Eclipse's pools | Otter-like broods: the young swim, the adults live on land. Eats endermites and drops a pearl. |
| **Mirror Wretch** | the Eclipse's border | Gaunt, addicted to Memory Moss, throws familiar sounds to lure you toward the edge, and hunts endermen - why they fear the Eclipse. |
| **The Curator** *(miniboss)* | the Leviathan | See above. |

Shulkers and Endermen (vanilla) fill out the rest. **Cut:** Cinder Mite, Live Comet, The Hollow.

## Blocks

- **One wood set: Nullwood.** Chorus Wood and its Chorus Bloom are cut (chorus is vanilla chorus
  again); Lumine keeps only its cap and stem, like vanilla's huge mushrooms, and lives in the Eclipse.
- **Stone:** Nightstone, Pale End Stone, Sunscorched Stone (Eclipse only), their families.
- **Bone:** vanilla bone block for every skeleton; new blocks only for what was built onto them.
- **New:** Memory Moss, Mimic Plant, Breathcomb, the Eclipse's turf and water plants, the Wellspring,
  purpur walls and purpur bricks.
- **Kept:** Upwells and Upwell Motes, Voidgel, End Chimes, Glimmer Crystal (recoloured), Void Crystal,
  Meteoric Iron, Solarite (Eclipse only), Lodestar (now an End respawn point).

## Starforging

An Astral Forge upgrades diamond and netherite gear. **One upgrade per item, five to choose from, all
sidegrades** - none simply better, none a new tier. Each upgrade has a **constellation**, which you must
first sight through a spyglass from the Eclipse, and a **rare material** from somewhere else, so every
upgrade is a journey. The forge's screen draws the constellation as you forge.

| Upgrade | Constellation | Rare material |
|---|---|---|
| Void Recall | the Leviathan | Curator's Core (the Leviathan) |
| Echo Strike | the Maw | Rift Pearl (waking a Rift Maw) |
| Lensing | the Watcher | Cracked Lens (Void Wanderer) |
| Radiant | the Remnant | Bottled Flare (the Eclipse's hell half, in a Sunstorm) |
| Rift Walker | the Whale | Whalelight Scale (shed by a tamed Drift Whale) |

Starforge Alloy (bound with Dragon's Breath) stays, a little cheaper. Time Stretch and Heat Shield go.
The **Constellation Mazes** stay: sighting a constellation from the Eclipse reveals where one is, and
its bridges only show through a spyglass.

## Atmosphere

- **Sky:** vanilla's black, starless sky, drifting very slowly and tinted faintly by the biome. No sun,
  no moon, no stars, no auroras, no comets - except over the Eclipse. Now and then a **void streak**.
- **Void hum:** within a few blocks of an island edge, a low rising drone - atmosphere and warning.
- **Ambience:** each biome its own quiet loop and occasional sounds (done).
- **Music:** four tracks and a disc (done); the warm Heavenly Loop belongs to the Eclipse; the central
  island stays quiet.
- **No text:** the Rift Maw and maze messages become sound, particles and light.
- **The Eclipse's ground follows vanilla's End:** bare End Stone in the heaven half (no ground cover;
  moss only round its water), the hell half's crust crimson, every texture within vanilla's End range
  (few tones, low saturation, little contrast), and its flora sparse.
- **The Eclipse is the one place with colour:** crimson over its hell half and teal-cyan over its heaven
  half, in its auroras, fog and water (after the Horsehead Nebula's glow and the blue reflection
  nebula at its foot). Everywhere else stays muted. The colour comes from light, not blocks, so the
  crimson doesn't read as the Nether's.
- **The Watcher's eye** is the Hourglass Nebula: red lobes, a rose ring, a glowing teal iris.

## Advancements

Rewritten around the new loop: sighting each constellation, swimming in the Wellspring over the void,
taming a Drift Whale by song, waking a Rift Maw, defeating the Curator, placing the egg on the
pedestal, setting a Lodestar respawn, being saved by Void Recall, following a Watcher to a leviathan.

## How we get there

Each phase passes the CI survey and playtests before the next begins. It's a beta without world
compatibility: testers need new worlds.

1. **Cuts and re-homing.**
   - Out of the world (code kept): the Resonant Spire and the Spire Wyrm.
   - Cut: the Comet Wake biome, live comets and comet showers, the Cinder Mite, the Live Comet,
     The Hollow, the Chorus and Lumine wood sets, Time Stretch and Heat Shield.
   - The general sky goes back to black (stars, auroras, constellations, comets and the Remnant only
     over the Corona, which becomes the Eclipse's hell half in phase 3).
   - Text messages become sound and light. Pollinators move to the Corona. Palette pass on Glimmer
     Crystal. Comet Craters move to the Ashen Barrens.
2. **Sparseness and caves.** Smaller islands with bigger gaps; the Crags' root caves; the Umbral
   Stalker moves into them; the Chorus Tangle and the Deep Hollows.
3. **The Eclipse.** The rare yin-yang biome, the Wellspring, its flora, the Remnant over its hell half,
   the Hushback, Hydragrip and Mirror Wretch.
4. **Systems.** Memory Moss, the Mimic Plant, Drift Whale pods and song taming, the Watcher as a guide,
   constellation-gated Starforging, the Lodestar respawn, purpur walls and bricks, the void hum and
   streaks.
5. **The Leviathan and the Curator.** The landmark, its garrison and Hollowbacks, the fight, the egg
   pedestal; then the advancements, the docs and a release.
