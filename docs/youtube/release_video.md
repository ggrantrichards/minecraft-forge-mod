# The Astral End - YouTube release video

A plan and full script for the beta.4 release video: what makes a mod showcase work, the script itself,
how to film each shot, and the title, thumbnail, description and Shorts to go with it.

Narration is your own voice (no synthetic TTS). Everything shown here is in beta.4; anything marked
**check first** should be tried in-game before you film it.

---

## 1. What a great mod release video does

1. **Hooks in the first 10 seconds.** Most viewers decide almost at once. Open on the single most
   striking thing - the Leviathan, 370 blocks of skeleton over the void - and a line that makes a
   promise. No intro, no logo, no "hey guys".
2. **Shows play, not just scenery.** Flyovers look nice, but people subscribe for *moments*: a chest
   opening sound that turns out to be the Mirror Wretch, the Watcher teleporting away the instant you
   look at it, a maze bridge that only exists through a spyglass, walking upside down on a ceiling.
   Every section needs at least one thing the player *does*.
3. **Has one spine.** The mod's spine is a journey outward: from the dragon's island, past 1,000 blocks,
   through the biomes, to the Leviathan, and back to the forge with what it kept. Each section should
   push the viewer further out.
4. **Opens loops.** Tease the big payoff early ("at the end of its spine there's a maze you can't see")
   and pay it off later. Change something every 20-40 seconds: a new place, a new sound, a cut to black.
5. **Uses the mod's own identity.** This mod says things with *sound and light*, not text. The video
   should too: let the game's silence, the void hum, the Wretch's stolen sounds and the music carry
   moments. Narrate less than feels natural.
6. **Is honest.** It's a beta: say so, say new worlds are needed, and don't film anything you had to
   fake. Viewers who install it should find what they saw.
7. **Ends with one clear ask.** Where to get it (Modrinth), what it needs (Minecraft 26.3, Forge), and
   what you want from them (feedback in the comments). Not five asks.

**Length:** about **7-8 minutes** for the main video (long enough for chapters and the Leviathan set
piece, short enough to hold). Cut a **75-second trailer** from it (the CI trailer in
`tools/trailer/scenes.txt` already is one), and **three Shorts** (section 6).

---

## 2. Title and thumbnail

Pick one title; they all lead with the experience, not the word "mod":

- **I Turned Minecraft's Empty End Into Something Alive**
- **The End Was Never Empty** (the mod's own line - best if the thumbnail does the work)
- **There's a 370-Block Leviathan Floating in My End**

**Thumbnail:** the Leviathan's skull from below and to one side, the player tiny on a ledge for scale,
its eye sockets lit. High contrast: the cream bone and purple purpur against black void. At most three
words, if any ("IT'S STILL BEATING" with the heart works). A second option: the Eclipse disc from
above, half teal and half crimson - nothing else in Minecraft looks like it.

## 3. The script

Times are approximate. **VO** is what you say. **ON SCREEN** is what we see. **DO** is what the player
does in the shot. Keep the music low under the VO and let it swell in the gaps.

### 0:00 - Cold open (hook) - 15 s

> **ON SCREEN:** Black. The void hum fades in. Cut to the Leviathan, side-on from far away, drifting,
> the whole skeleton in frame. Slow push in.
>
> **VO:** "This is the End. Not the one you know."
>
> **ON SCREEN:** Cut to the skull's eye sockets lighting up, one after another.
>
> **VO:** "Something out here is still alive. Let's find out what."

*Title card: The Astral End* (2 s, on black, no music sting - just the hum stopping).

### 0:15 - The premise - 30 s

> **ON SCREEN:** The vanilla main island and the dragon, then the camera rising and turning outward,
> over the end gateway, into the dark.
>
> **VO:** "In vanilla Minecraft, the End is the last stop. You kill the dragon, grab an elytra, and
> never really think about it again. I wanted it to feel like it was *waiting* for you."
>
> **ON SCREEN:** The outer islands coming into view: sparse, drifting, far apart.
>
> **VO:** "So past a thousand blocks out, the islands drift apart, and the End becomes its own place:
> quiet, strange, full of ruins nobody is left to explain. No new armour tiers, no villagers, no text
> popping up in your face. Everything it tells you, it tells you with sound and light."

### 0:45 - The islands - 1 min 15 s

One shot per biome; one line each. Don't explain mechanics yet, just show variety.

> **Nebula Fields** - walking across violet Chorus Moss, then a jump that floats too long.
> **VO:** "Nebula Fields. Low gravity - every jump is a little too long."
>
> **Chorus Tangle** - the camera threading through vanilla chorus groves.
> **VO:** "The Chorus Tangle: easy to get lost in, easier to fall out of."
>
> **Hollow Isles** - Nullwood forest on a ring island; then under it, the Upwell.
> **VO:** "The Hollow Isles - the only forest out here."
> **DO:** Step into an Upwell. The view rolls over; you land on the underside of the island and walk
> on it, head down. *(Let it play with no VO for 3 seconds.)*
> **VO:** "...and under them, the Upwells. Gravity isn't a rule here. It's a suggestion."
>
> **Hollow Roots** - inside the cave under the isles: glowcaps, hanging roots, amber Starsap, fireflies.
> **VO:** "Inside them, the Hollow Roots, where the dead trees' amber still glows."
>
> **Glimmering Crags / Shattered Crags** - Nightstone peaks, then the shards splitting over the void.
> **VO:** "The Glimmering Crags - and where they've shattered, geodes drift underneath."
>
> **Crystal Vaults** - inside a geode. A crystal cluster that turns out to be a Shardback unfolding.
> *(Silence; let the reveal land.)*
>
> **Ashen Barrens** - pale stone, old bones. **DO:** stand on Memory Moss and let it replay a sound.
> **VO:** "And the Ashen Barrens. A graveyard. The moss here remembers what passed over it."

### 2:00 - The Eclipse - 45 s

> **ON SCREEN:** From high above, the Eclipse: one great disc, half teal, half crimson. Slow descent.
>
> **VO:** "Then, far out, rare enough you might never see it: the Eclipse. Heaven and hell, turning
> round each other."
>
> **ON SCREEN:** The heaven half - bare End Stone, the Wellspring, the teal sky. Then cross the line
> into the hell half - crimson stone, a Sunstorm, the Remnant hanging overhead.
>
> **VO:** "One half still and quiet. The other burning, under what's left of a dead star."
>
> **ON SCREEN:** Night in the Eclipse; the stars visible without a spyglass.
>
> **VO:** "It's the only place out here you can see the stars. Remember that - you'll need them."
> *(This is your first open loop: it pays off in the Starforge section.)*

### 2:45 - The creatures - 1 min 30 s

Each creature gets a behaviour, not just a turntable.

> **Drift Whales** - a pod passing overhead, a calf beside its mother, one rolling.
> **VO:** "Drift Whales travel in pods - no two alike. They hum to each other. They play."
> **DO:** Hold a note block or Chorus Fruit near one; it answers and lets you ride. **(check first:
> whale riding has a known open issue - film it only if it works smoothly.)**
>
> **Hushbacks** - a herd grazing. **DO:** hit one; the herd blinks away.
> **VO:** "Hushbacks. Hurt one, and the whole herd is just... gone."
>
> **Hydragrips** - a newt lit with cyan, hunting an endermite by the water; it coughs up an ender pearl.
> **VO:** "Hydragrips hunt endermites - and every one they eat, they cough back up as a pearl."
>
> **The Mirror Wretch** - this is the scare beat. Film it in first person, at an island edge, quiet.
> **DO:** You hear a chest open somewhere near the edge. You turn. Nothing. A door. Footsteps. You walk
> toward it - and the Wretch rears up over the edge, two blocks tall, and strikes.
> **VO (after, quieter):** "The Mirror Wretch doesn't have a face. It has every voice it's ever heard -
> and it uses them to call you to the edge."
>
> **The Watcher** - far off on a ridge, in the corner of the frame. **DO:** turn to look straight at it.
> It teleports away. Pan further along - it's there again, further on.
> **VO:** "And the Watcher. Don't look straight at it. Keep it in the corner of your eye... and follow."
> *(Second open loop: where is it leading you? Cut straight to the next section.)*

### 4:15 - The Leviathan - 2 min 15 s (the set piece)

> **ON SCREEN:** The Watcher on a ridge, then the camera following its gaze up: the Leviathan, high
> over the void.
>
> **VO:** "It leads you here. The whole skeleton of a Star Leviathan, three hundred and seventy blocks
> from tail to chin. And someone built a citadel on it."
>
> **ON SCREEN:** A flight along the spine: the purpur hall, the towers on the vertebrae, shulkers.
>
> **VO:** "A hall down its spine. Towers on its bones."
>
> **The maze.** **DO:** climb the last tower. Empty air between platforms. Raise the spyglass - star
> bridges appear. Cross them.
> **VO:** "On the last tower, a Constellation Maze. The bridges are there. You just can't see them -
> unless you look through a spyglass."
>
> **The heart.** **ON SCREEN:** Inside the ribcage, the crimson heart beating, the Breathcomb lungs
> glowing. *(Let the heartbeat sound play alone for a moment.)*
> **VO:** "Inside, it's still alive. Its heart still beats. Its lungs still breathe light."
>
> **The Curator.** The reliquary cathedral, the Curator rising. A real fight - show at least three of:
> it teleporting away from an arrow, smashing an End Chimes pylon, slamming you into the air over the
> void, flinching from water.
> **VO (sparse, between hits):** "The ribcage is a cathedral now. And it has a keeper." ... "It
> smashes the chimes that hold it." ... "It hates water."
>
> **The egg.** **DO:** set the dragon egg on the pedestal under the heart. Cut outside: the skull's
> eyes light.
> **VO:** "Bring it the dragon's egg..."
>
> **The Maw.** Inside the skull: teeth like trees, the brain hall, the throat waking into a wormhole.
> **DO:** step in; arrive at another Leviathan.
> **VO:** "...and the skull wakes. Its throat is a road - to every other Leviathan you've woken."

### 6:30 - Starforging - 45 s

> **ON SCREEN:** Back home, the Astral Forge. Then the Eclipse at night, a constellation through the
> spyglass. *(Payoff of the open loop from the Eclipse section.)*
>
> **VO:** "What you bring back goes into the Astral Forge. Five upgrades for diamond and netherite
> gear - not stronger, just different. And each one needs its constellation, sighted from the Eclipse."
>
> **ON SCREEN:** One quick demo each, a second or two apiece: Rift Walker boots making tiles under your
> feet over the void; Void Recall putting you back after a fall; Echo Strike's second hit chiming;
> Radiant making a target glow; Lensing.
>
> **VO:** "Walk on the void. Fall into it and come back."

### 7:15 - Small things, and the ask - 30 s

> **ON SCREEN:** A fast montage, no VO, music up: Hollow Thorn carving a pumpkin into a violet Hollow
> Lantern; a Lodestar respawn; the music disc in a jukebox; the void hum at an island edge; a Void
> Wanderer studying you and leaving a Chart Fragment.
>
> **ON SCREEN:** The Leviathan from far off again, as in the cold open (bookend).
>
> **VO:** "The Astral End is out now on Modrinth, for Minecraft 26.3 on Forge. It's a beta - start a
> new world, keep backups - and tell me in the comments what you find out there. I want to know what
> it feels like to you."
>
> *Logo card. Hold on the hum. Cut to black.*

## 4. Filming it

**Settings:** 1080p60 (or 1440p), FOV 70 or a little under for cinematic shots, F1 to hide the HUD,
smooth camera for flyovers, render distance as high as your machine holds (the Leviathan needs 12+
chunks). Record game audio and your voice on separate tracks.

**Getting there fast** (in a test world with cheats on):

| What | Command |
| --- | --- |
| A Leviathan | `/locate structure astralend:leviathan` |
| A Constellation Maze | `/locate structure astralend:constellation_maze` |
| The Remnant | `/locate structure astralend:remnant` |
| A biome | `/locate biome astralend:eclipse` (also `nebula_fields`, `hollow_isles`, `hollow_roots`, `glimmering_crags`, `shattered_crags`, `crystal_vaults`, `ashen_barrens`, `chorus_thicket`, `corona`) |
| A creature, for close-ups | its spawn egg (a summoned Watcher stays put) |

**Two ways to get smooth camera moves:**
- The mod's own cinematic recorder (what CI uses for the trailer): `tools/trailer/scenes.txt` lists
  each shot, and CI films it on a commit with `[trailer]` in its message. Use it for flyovers and orbits.
- Play the gameplay beats yourself (the Wretch scare, the maze, the Curator fight, the Upwell, the
  whale ride) - those need to feel like play, not a camera.

**Order:** film the gameplay beats first (they're the hardest to get right), then the flyovers.
Record VO last, to the cut.

**Sound:** the mod's music is free to use: four CC0 tracks and one CC-BY 3.0 track, which needs credit
in the description (below). Let silence work - the cold open, the Wretch scare and the heartbeat are
stronger with no music at all.

## 6. Shorts (vertical, 20-40 s each)

1. **"Don't follow the sound."** First person at an island edge. A chest opens somewhere. Footsteps.
   You walk toward it. The Mirror Wretch rears up. Cut. Caption only: *"It only knows the voices it's
   stolen."*
2. **"Don't look at it."** The Watcher at the edge of the frame. You look; it's gone. You look further
   along; it's there. It leads you to the Leviathan's skull. Caption: *"It's leading you somewhere."*
3. **"It's still beating."** Push into the ribcage to the heart, heartbeat audio only, then pull all
   the way out to the full Leviathan. Caption: *"370 blocks. Still alive."*

Each Short ends on the mod's name and "on Modrinth" - no other call to action.

## 7. Before you publish

- [ ] Every feature shown works in beta.4 as filmed (try the whale ride first).
- [ ] Nothing in the video was faked or edited to behave differently than it does in-game.
- [ ] The hook is under 15 seconds, and something new happens every 20-40 seconds after it.
- [ ] VO is clear over the music; silence where the moment carries itself.
- [ ] The description has the Modrinth link, the version, the beta warning and the CC-BY credit.
- [ ] The thumbnail reads at phone size.
